Event Inquiry & Quote Automation

1

Deploy AI to handle initial event inquiries 24/7—gathering event details (date, location, guest count, service type), checking staff availability, and generating instant quotes. Prospective clients receive immediate responses rather than waiting for business hours, while you capture all inquiry details in a structured format. The system can handle follow-up questions, send quote reminders, and escalate complex requests to your team.

Staff Scheduling & Coordination

2

Automate the entire staff booking workflow—from initial shift offers to confirmations and backup alerts. When an event is confirmed, the system automatically identifies qualified staff based on skills (bartending, silver service, mixology), location proximity, and availability. Staff receive shift offers via their preferred channel, can accept/decline with one tap, and the system automatically moves to the next candidate if needed. Last-minute cancellations trigger automatic replacement searches.

Staff Pool Communication

£5K – £7K/year

Centralise and automate mass communication with your staff pool. Send availability requests, training updates, and opportunities to segmented groups based on skills, location, or experience level. Track responses automatically and maintain engagement with inactive staff through periodic check-ins.

Relevant Departments: Operations, HR, Staff Relations

Commission/Payment Tracking

£4K – £5K/year

Automate the calculation and tracking of staff payments based on hours worked, event type, and role complexity. The system logs shift completions, calculates pay including any bonuses or deductions, and generates payment reports. Staff can view their earnings in real-time, reducing payment queries.

Relevant Departments: Finance, Operations, HR

Staff Availability Management

£4K – £5K/year

Enable staff to maintain their own availability calendars through a simple interface. The system proactively requests availability updates for busy periods (wedding season, Christmas), aggregates data for capacity planning, and alerts you to potential staffing gaps before they become problems.

Relevant Departments: Operations, Staff Coordination

Post-Event Feedback Collection

£2K – £3K/year

Gather structured feedback from both clients and staff after each event. Automated surveys capture service quality insights, identify top performers, flag issues for review, and build a knowledge base of what works for different event types and venues.

Relevant Departments: Operations, Quality Assurance, HR

Event Briefing Automation

£3K – £4K/year

Automatically generate and distribute event briefing documents to assigned staff before each event. Include venue details, dress code, arrival times, contact information, and special instructions. Staff acknowledge receipt, and the system flags any who haven't reviewed their briefing 24 hours before the event.

Relevant Departments: Operations, Event Delivery
