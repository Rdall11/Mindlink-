Task Ownership Format

Every task MUST include:

  1. Owner: Who's responsible (Cyril or Pius)
  2. Outcome: Specific, measurable deliverable (not just "work on X")
  3. Deadline: Day of the week, not vague "end of week"
✓ Good Example:
Owner: Cyril | Outcome: 20 partnership outreach emails sent with tracking spreadsheet | Due: Wednesday
✗ Bad Example:
Owner: Cyril | Outcome: Work on partnerships | Due: This week

Daily Coordination

Morning Check-In (Optional but Recommended)

Time: 9:30 AM GMT

Format: Quick 5-minute sync in Slack or voice

  • What are you working on today?
  • Any blockers?
  • Need anything from the other person?

Blocker Protocol

If you're blocked:

  1. Post in #questions-escalations immediately (don't wait)
  2. Tag the person who can unblock you
  3. Include: What you're blocked on + What you need to proceed

If someone else is blocked:

  1. Respond within 2 hours during working hours
  2. If you can't unblock them, escalate to R with context

Coordination Between Cyril & Pius

Handoff Points

Cyril → Pius:

  • When partnership/influencer responds positively → hand warm lead to Pius for nurturing
  • Share successful messaging templates that work

Pius → Cyril:

  • When target audience member asks about partnerships → route to Cyril
  • Share engagement insights (what content resonates) for company page

Weekly Sync (Recommended)

Time: Wednesday 2 PM GMT (mid-week checkpoint)

Duration: 15 minutes

Agenda:

  • Quick update on progress
  • Share wins and learnings
  • Coordinate any handoffs
  • Surface blockers early

Cyril's Metrics:

  • Partnership outreach: # contacted, # responses, # meetings
  • Influencer outreach: # contacted, # responses, # collaborations
  • LinkedIn company page: impressions, clicks, followers gained, engagement rate
  • Content posted: # posts, best performing post

Pius's Metrics:

  • Conversations: # started, # ongoing, # converted to meetings
  • Engagement: # comments posted, # DMs sent, response rate
  • Facebook page: setup milestones, initial followers

Tracking: Shared Google Sheet updated weekly (link in Slack channel description)

Questions & Escalations

When to Ask R for Direction:

Strategic Questions (ask in Monday priorities post):

  • Target audience clarification
  • Messaging/positioning changes
  • New initiative ideas
  • Budget/tool requests

Urgent Questions (ask immediately in Slack):

  • Approval needed for external commitments
  • Brand/legal concerns
  • Something broke

Non-Urgent Questions (batch for weekly sync with R):

  • Process improvements
  • Long-term planning
  • Career development

Standard Operating Procedures

Monday Morning (Intern Manager):

  1. Review last week's outcomes vs. priorities
  2. Draft this week's priorities based on R's direction
  3. Ensure each task has Owner + Outcome + Deadline
  4. Post by 10 AM GMT
  5. Answer any clarifying questions

Wednesday Mid-Week (Intern Manager):

  1. Check progress on priorities
  2. Unblock anyone who's stuck
  3. Adjust priorities if needed
  4. Ensure coordination is happening

Friday Afternoon (Intern Manager):

  1. Collect metrics from both interns
  2. Write end-of-week summary
  3. Post by 5 PM GMT
  4. Identify next week's focus areas

Success Criteria

Weekly priorities are effective when:

  • Each priority has clear owner, outcome, deadline
  • No more than 5 priorities per person
  • At least 80% of priorities are completed
  • Blockers are identified and resolved within 24 hours

Team coordination is working when:

  • No duplicate work happening
  • Handoffs happen smoothly
  • Each person knows what the other is working on
  • Blockers are caught early (Wednesday checkpoint)

Getting Started Checklist

  • Intern manager posts first Monday priorities using template
  • Both interns acknowledge and ask clarifying questions
  • Set up shared metrics tracking spreadsheet
  • Schedule optional Wednesday sync time
  • Test blocker protocol once to ensure everyone understands
  • First Friday summary posted to establish rhythm

Notes for Intern Manager

Your role is:

  • Translate R's strategic direction into weekly priorities
  • Ensure tasks are specific and measurable
  • Unblock the interns quickly
  • Track progress and report up to R
  • Facilitate coordination between Cyril and Pius

You are NOT responsible for:

  • Doing the work yourself
  • Being available 24/7
  • Making strategic decisions (escalate to R)
  • Micromanaging - trust the interns to execute

When in doubt:

  • Make the task more specific
  • Add the deadline
  • Ask R for clarity
  • Document the decision
