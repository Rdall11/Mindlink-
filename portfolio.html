How We Work
1

Discovery Call

A 30-minute conversation where we map out your current operations, identify bottlenecks, and understand what success looks like. We'll discuss your team structure, existing tools, and the processes that consume the most time or cause the most friction.

2

Comprehensive Audit

We conduct a deep analysis of your workflows across departments. You receive a detailed report including: an AI readiness assessment, 8+ prioritized automation opportunities ranked by impact and effort, projected annual savings for each opportunity, and a visual matrix to guide decision-making. This becomes your automation roadmap.

3

Implementation

We build and deploy the automations you choose to move forward with. This includes workflow design, system integrations with your existing tools (CRM, ERP, communication platforms), custom AI solutions where needed, and thorough testing. We work in sprints with regular check-ins so you see progress and can provide feedback throughout.

4

Handoff & Support

Every automation comes with complete documentation and team training so your staff can manage day-to-day operations confidently. We provide 30 days of post-launch support to address any issues, and offer ongoing maintenance packages for businesses that want continuous optimization as they scale.
