How We Work
01

Discovery Call

A focused conversation where we map out your current operations, identify friction points, and understand what success looks like for your business. We discuss your team structure, existing systems, and the processes consuming the most time or resources.

02

Comprehensive Audit

We conduct a deep analysis of your workflows, speak with stakeholders across different departments, and examine your existing systems and how they interconnect. We also take into account your work culture and how your team actually operates day-to-day. From this, we build a detailed AI Automation Audit: a strategic report with prioritized opportunities, projected savings, and a clear implementation roadmap.

03

Implementation

We build and deploy the automations you choose to move forward with. This includes workflow design, system integrations with your existing tools, custom AI solutions where needed, and thorough testing. We work in focused sprints with regular check-ins so you see progress and can provide feedback throughout the process.

04

Handoff & Support

Every automation comes with complete documentation and team training. We provide 30 days of post-launch support, and offer ongoing maintenance packages for businesses that want continuous optimization as they scale.
