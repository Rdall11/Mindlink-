Recommended Next Steps

  1. Discovery Workshop (Week 1-2) - Deep dive into your current inquiry handling, scheduling process, and staff communication methods. Map the exact workflows, identify integration points, and define success metrics specific to Ruby Reign Events.
  2. Priority #1: Inquiry Automation (Weeks 3-8) - Implement automated inquiry response and quote generation. Test with real inquiries while running parallel to your existing process, then cut over once confidence is high.
  3. Priority #2: Scheduling System (Weeks 8-16) - Deploy intelligent staff scheduling with availability matching and shift offers. Start with a subset of events to validate the workflow, then expand to all bookings.
  4. Priority #3: Staff Communication Hub (Weeks 14-26) - Centralise all staff communication and event briefings. This is the foundation that makes everything else more powerful. Roll out gradually, starting with your most engaged staff members.
  5. Measure & Expand (Month 7+) - Review results from the first three initiatives. Use learnings to prioritise remaining opportunities: Review Collection, Post-Event Follow-ups, and Availability Tracking based on observed impact.
