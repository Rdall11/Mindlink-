AI Automation Audit

Find Hidden Efficiency & Unlock ROI in Your Operations

What is an AI Automation Audit?

A diagnostic assessment of your operations that identifies high-value automation opportunities, quantifies ROI, and delivers a prioritized implementation roadmap.

The Challenge: Growing portfolios create complexity. Your team is buried in manual work. You need to scale efficiently—but where do you start?

What We Analyze

  • Current workflows & processes
  • Time spent on manual vs strategic work
  • System/tool fragmentation
  • Data readiness for AI implementation
  • Process documentation maturity

What You Receive

  • AI Readiness Score (1-10 assessment)
  • 6-10 automation opportunities identified
  • ROI projections (time saved + cost impact)
  • Visual opportunity matrix (effort vs impact)
  • Prioritized implementation roadmap

The Process

We conduct discovery calls and interviews with key stakeholders across your departments—operations, finance, investor relations, and leadership. Through these conversations, we build a comprehensive strategic view of your workflows, pain points, and automation opportunities. The depth of discovery is tailored to your organization's complexity and needs.

What Happens After?

You choose how to proceed:

  • Implement yourself — Use the roadmap with your internal team or contractors
  • Build together — We handle implementation ($10K-$80K depending on scope)
  • Plan for later — Keep the roadmap for when timing is right
Why This Matters: Don't invest in automation blindly. Get a clear roadmap tailored to your operations—with quantified savings and risk assessment—before committing resources.
