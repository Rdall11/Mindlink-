What is an AI Automation Audit?

A comprehensive diagnostic assessment of your business operations to identify high-value automation opportunities. We analyze your workflows, calculate ROI potential, and deliver a prioritized roadmap—so you know exactly where AI can help before investing a single dollar.

The Problem: Growing portfolios create operational complexity. Your team is buried in manual tasks. Hiring is expensive. You need to scale efficiently—but where do you start?

What We Analyze

  • Current workflows & processes
  • Time spent on manual vs strategic work
  • System/tool fragmentation
  • Data readiness for AI implementation
  • Process documentation maturity

What You Receive

  • AI Readiness Score (1-10 assessment)
  • 6-10 automation opportunities identified
  • ROI projections (time saved + cost impact)
  • Visual opportunity matrix (effort vs impact)
  • Prioritized implementation roadmap
  • Timeline: 1-2 weeks from kickoff to delivery

Who This is For

Ideal for: PE firms managing €20M+ in assets • 5-15 person teams feeling stretched • Growing portfolios (10+ properties) • Spending >20 hrs/week on admin tasks that could be automated

What Happens After the Audit?

Three paths forward:

  • DIY Implementation: Take the roadmap and build internally or with contractors
  • Build Together: We handle the entire implementation (typical: $10K-$80K depending on scope) ⭐ Most popular
  • Future Planning: Keep the roadmap for later when timing is better—no pressure
Why This Matters: Without an audit, you're guessing which processes to automate and what the ROI will be. With an audit, you get a clear roadmap based on YOUR operations—quantified savings, risk assessment, and team buy-in before investing in implementation.

Simple 4-Step Process

1. Book kickoff call (30 min) — we ask questions about your workflows
2. We deliver audit report (1-2 weeks)
3. Review findings call (30 min) — walk through recommendations together
4. Decide next steps — implement, DIY, or table it for later
