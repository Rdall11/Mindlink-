Executive Summary

Your organization is a high-performing luxury residential lease brokerage generating $20M in annual revenue with an impressive 60% profit margin. You've built strong relationships with enterprise clients—your top 10 accounts represent 70% of revenue—and you're well-positioned for growth. However, your operational infrastructure hasn't scaled with the business.

We've identified $460,000–$690,000 in annual savings potential across 8 automation opportunities. The core challenges center on three areas: visibility lag (month-end reporting takes 20 days), scattered data (Google Sheets, Drive, and DealCore operating in silos), and person-dependent execution (communication via email, personal phones, and verbal approvals creates continuity risk).

Your stated objective—to become an "AI-enabled leasing and renewals operator" with a predictable 60-day renewal engine—is achievable within 12 months. We recommend starting with three high-impact initiatives: automated renewal pipeline tracking, digital approval workflows, and a centralized communication hub. These will address your core pain points while building the foundation for broader automation.

Commission & Revenue Reconciliation Automation

$60K – $90K/year

Automate your month-end commission calculations and revenue reconciliation, reducing your current 20-day lag to same-day reporting. The system handles partner-specific commission rules (8-10% for new leases, 4-5% for renewals) automatically, flagging discrepancies for human review rather than requiring manual calculation across spreadsheets.

Relevant Departments: Finance, Leadership, Account Management

Document Management & Version Control

$50K – $70K/year

Replace your scattered Google Drive folder structure with a centralized document management system that automatically organizes contracts, quotes, and lease documents by partner, property, and transaction. Version control ensures everyone works from the latest document, and search functionality eliminates time spent hunting for files.

Relevant Departments: Transaction Management, Compliance, Account Management

Transaction Milestone Automation

$60K – $90K/year

Automate the tracking and coordination of transaction milestones—document collection, signature status, handoff triggers, and deadline monitoring. Clear ownership is assigned at each stage, eliminating the "waiting on something and it's not clear who owns it" problem. Automated notifications keep all parties aligned without manual follow-up.

Relevant Departments: Transaction Management, Placement, Finance

Partner/Account Health Dashboard

$30K – $50K/year

Create real-time visibility into your top 10 accounts (70% of revenue) with automated health scoring, SLA tracking, and early warning indicators. Leadership can see portfolio performance without "asking five people," and Account Management can proactively address issues before they escalate.

Relevant Departments: Account Management, Leadership, Portfolio Partnerships

Quote Generation & Pricing Automation

$40K – $60K/year

AI-assisted quote generation that pulls pricing guidelines, concession bands, and market data to create accurate quotes faster. Recommended Next Steps

  1. Discovery Workshop (Week 1-2) — Deep dive into your renewal pipeline and approval workflows with Transaction Management and Account Management teams. Validate assumptions, map current-state processes in detail, and define success metrics.
  2. Priority #1: Renewal Dashboard (Weeks 3-10) — Implement automated renewal tracking with 60-day alerts. Start with your top 10 accounts to prove value quickly, then expand across the portfolio.
  3. Priority #2: Approval Workflows (Weeks 8-14) — Deploy digital approval system for exceptions. Run parallel to existing process for 2 weeks, then cut over. Train all approvers on new workflow.
  4. Priority #3: Communication Hub (Weeks 12-26) — Longer implementation but foundational. Begin with Account Management team, expand to Placement, then full rollout. Requires change management focus.
  5. Measure & Expand (Month 7+) — Review results from first three initiatives. Use learnings to prioritize next wave: Commission Automation, Document Management, or Transaction Milestone system based on observed pain points.
